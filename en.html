About Me

I am an Associate Professor at the School of Information and Electrical Engineering, Shandong Jianzhu University, and currently Head of the Intelligent Technology Teaching and Research Section. I received my Ph.D. from the School of Electronic Information, Wuhan University in 2013, after which I worked on UAV and missile-borne data-link technologies at the 54th Research Institute of China Electronics Technology Group Corporation, and later led wireless network protocol, information security architecture and industry standards work at Hisense Smart Home. I have been teaching at Shandong Jianzhu University since 2019. My current research and teaching focus on communication and measurement & control, digital signal processing and FPGA implementation, IoT and edge computing with deep-learning hardware acceleration, time series analysis, and intelligent building equipment and energy-efficiency management.

I have a solid theoretical foundation and extensive engineering experience in signal processing and communication engineering. I began systematic training in electronic design in my sophomore year and took part in a number of advanced-research topics and type-model projects as a student. In industry I served as project leader on two advanced-research topics, and as a key contributor I helped advance the National Development and Reform Commission project on smart-home terminal product development and a demonstration platform for operational scenarios. I have more than 20 years of FPGA and embedded-system development experience and am familiar with the full lifecycle of industrial project development.

Research Areas

Communication & Measurement and Control · Digital Signal Processing and FPGA Implementation
  • High-speed demodulation and equalisation: iterative equalisation, joint channel estimation and Turbo equalisation, frequency-domain Turbo equalisation, and parallel high-speed demodulation algorithms (3 GSPS sampling, fully FPGA-based demodulation of 800 Mbps QPSK IF signals with CCSDS-compatible descrambling, decoding and frame synchronisation).
  • Digital down-conversion and signal recognition: optimisation and implementation of general-purpose multi-channel digital down-converters, IF processing for spectrum monitoring receivers, and automatic modulation recognition of visible light communication signals.
  • Hardware acceleration and engineering implementation: on-board FPGA programming and debugging, hardware schematic design and system integration testing; more than 30 years of FPGA development experience.
IoT & Edge Computing · Deep-Learning Hardware Acceleration
  • Wireless networks and protocols: clustering algorithms for heterogeneous self-organising networks in smart homes, congestion control for wireless multi-router networks based on adaptive integral backstepping (CCAIB), priority-based congestion control for the IoT, and multi-channel D2D downlink resource sharing.
  • Edge computing and intelligent forecasting: cloud-edge collaborative transfer prediction of building energy consumption, KAN-based delay-aware dynamic graph networks for traffic flow forecasting, and self-learning edge-weight graph convolutional networks for user energy-use classification.
  • Embedded AI and applications: engineering deployment on the Ascend AI platform and on GD32 embedded AI (driver drowsiness detection and warning, embedded system development, and related work).
Intelligent Building Equipment & Energy-Efficiency Management · Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring
  • Non-intrusive load monitoring (NILM): a survey paper (Acta Automatica Sinica), event detection based on an improved kNN algorithm and transient/steady-state features, parallel load disaggregation with temporal convolutional networks, and the design and application of a monitoring system fusing high- and low-frequency data.
  • Building energy use and equipment management: prediction of energy-use behaviour in office buildings (MCMC), multi-unit collaborative optimisation of ground-source heat pump air-conditioning systems, and fault detection and root-cause identification for HVAC systems.
  • Intelligent construction and prefabricated components: reinforcement-learning-based scheduling optimisation for prefabricated component production, grouped production scheduling for concrete prefabricated components, and safety warning for workers intruding into edge zones based on video trajectory analysis.

Engineering Projects

  • High-Speed Data Receiver (HDR) (2011.01 — 2013.03): a modular, integrated solution for high-speed satellite ground reception, with 3 GSPS high-speed sampling and an all-FPGA design, achieving demodulation, CCSDS-compatible descrambling/decoding and frame synchronisation of 800 Mbps QPSK IF signals. Responsible for scheme design, hardware debugging, parallel high-speed demodulation algorithm simulation and FPGA implementation, and performance testing.
  • Receiving and Demodulation Subsystem of a Satellite Video Surveillance Ground Checkout System (2011.04 — 2011.12): IF sampling and variable-rate BPSK/QPSK demodulation, Viterbi decoding, de-interleaving, descrambling and RS decoding, with data distributed over a network interface to the decryption and quick-look subsystems. Responsible for equipment scheme design, hardware debugging, main FPGA program design and debugging, and on-site integration testing.
  • Development of a Prototype Through-Wall Detection Radar (2009.09 — 2011.06): a radar prototype with a single-transmit dual-receive antenna system. Responsible for system hardware architecture design and part of the debugging, research on denoising algorithms and their FPGA implementation, and participation in field tests.
  • HY-2 Laser Communication Data Processing Terminal Equipment (2010.08 — 2011.03): high-speed laser signal acquisition and baseband signal analysis, providing data source code storage, download and subscription services.
  • ZY-3 Data Preprocessing Equipment (2009.08 — 2009.12): receives baseband data over Gigabit Ethernet and unpacks it; frame synchronisation, descrambling and RS decoding are performed on the preprocessing motherboard via a PCI interface; single-channel real-time processing rate > 600 Mbps.
  • Development of an IF Processing Board for a Spectrum Monitoring Receiver (2007.12 — 2009.11): 21.4 MHz IF high-speed sampling, digital down-conversion, spectrum analysis, and modulation scheme and parameter identification, together with demodulation of common analogue and digital modulated signals. Responsible for requirements analysis, system scheme design, hardware schematic design and on-board FPGA program development.
  • Laser Waveform Digitisation Device (2009.03 — 2009.07): a 3 GSPS sampling device that acquires narrow-pulse laser waveforms in real time and buffers them for upload. Responsible for hardware schematic design, system hardware debugging, and on-board FPGA program design and testing.
